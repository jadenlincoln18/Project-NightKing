### Verdict / Conclusion
> **"Tight in the body, with a real tail."**

NYMEX CL is an adequate hedge for Kalshi ICE-settled brackets on a current-account basis in the body of the distribution — no ICE licence needed. The tail risk (1 event per ~162 settlements reaching $4.70 basis) is real, unhedgeable by construction, and must inform position sizing. The 4 roll-boundary events are mechanically explained and manageable. The 2 unexplained events (especially the −$4.70 on 2026-06-15) are either a Kalshi settlement anomaly or a genuine ICE dislocation — these need further investigation before large positions are taken.

### Data Quality Issues / Concerns

- Roll-boundary assignments are a systematic artifact of Kalshi's calendar convention; manageable but must be modeled
- The `ts_ref` field in Databento statistics records is midnight UTC (not ET) — requires careful timezone handling to avoid date-off-by-one errors
- 2026-04-03 data is missing (no NYMEX settlement on that date — likely holiday/gap)

## 3. `data_cme/FINDINGS_CHAIN.md` — CME WTI Option Root Selection & TBBO Quote Density

### Purpose
Resolves two previously unverified items: (1) which CME WTI option roots to use for the hedge — LO (monthly) and the weeklies (LO1-5, ML1-5, WL1-5, MCO), and (2) whether the TBBO (top-of-book bid/offer) quote data is dense enough near the 14:30 ET settlement to calibrate an options smile without needing the higher-resolution bbo-1m feed. Generated 2026-09-10. Total data cost: **$14.60**.

### Key Numerical Results

**Option roots evaluated**: 17 total — LO (monthly), LO1-5 (Friday weeklies), ML1-5 (Monday weeklies), WL1-5 (Wednesday weeklies), MCO (micro crude options)

**Expiries per root in the Kalshi window**:
- LO: 23 expiries, 271 strikes/expiry (median), range $0.50–$500
- MCO: 12 expiries, 392 strikes/expiry, range $5–$210
- Weekly roots (LO1-5, ML1-5, WL1-5): 3–10 expiries each, 143–173 strikes/expiry

**KXWTI settlement day coverage**:
- 134 settlement dates; 83 have a CME option expiring that same day (62%)
- By weekday: 100% of Mondays (26/26), Wednesdays (27/27), and Fridays (25/26) have a co-expiry; Tuesdays (2/28) and Thursdays (3/27) almost never do — those days can only use the monthly LO

**KXWTIW settlement day coverage**:
- 37 settlement dates; 34 have a CME option expiring that day (92%)
- Nearly all KXWTIW settlements are Fridays; Friday LO weeklies (LO1-5) cover them well

**TBBO density results**:
- **KXWTI**: 79 of 83 option-expiry dates clear the 8-strike / both-wings / 60-minute threshold = **95% pass rate**
- **KXWTIW**: 30 of 34 dates clear = **88% pass rate**
- **Combined**: 109 of 117 dates pass = **93% pass rate**
- Failures (4 KXWTI + 4 KXWTIW):
  - 2026-04-16 MCO — only 2 below / 14 above (asymmetric, fails)
  - 2026-05-15 LO3 — only 1 above (fails)
  - 2026-06-16 MCO — only 1 below / 3 above (fails)
  - 2026-08-14 LO2 — only 2 above (fails)
  - 2026-08-31 ML5 — only 1 above (fails)
  - 2026-09-11 LO2 — 0 quotes (data ends 2026-09-10; expected)
  - 2026-01-02 LO1 — only 2 above (fails; early in dataset)

### Verdict / Conclusion
> **"TBBO is sufficient — no bbo-1m upgrade needed."**

The full set of 17 roots should be used: **LO, LO1, LO2, LO3, LO4, LO5, MCO, ML1, ML2, ML3, ML4, ML5, WL1, WL2, WL3, WL4, WL5.** CME lists weeklies expiring Monday (ML), Wednesday (WL), and Friday (LO); there are no Tuesday or Thursday weeklies, so KXWTI daily settlements on those days can only be hedged with the monthly LO on its own expiry day.

93% of Kalshi settlement dates have a co-expiring CME option with sufficient TBBO quote density to fit an implied vol smile. The 7% failure rate is driven by MCO (micro crude — consistently thinner) and a few near-market-close sparsity events, not a systemic data problem. The standard TBBO feed is adequate; upgrading to bbo-1m is not needed for the smile calibration step.

### Data Quality Issues / Concerns
- **MCO fails disproportionately** — only 2 of its 6 available dates clearly pass (2026-03-17 and 2026-05-14). MCO quotes are consistently older (median 94–165 minutes before settlement) vs. LOx/MLx/WLx (median 17–88 minutes). MCO may need special handling or exclusion from the smile fit.
- **2026-09-11 has zero TBBO quotes** for LO2 — this is the dataset cutoff date (generated 2026-09-10), not a real data issue.
- **2026-01-02 LO1 is marginally thin** — only 23 quotes total, 7 within 30 minutes; dataset is sparse at the beginning.
- The parquet store (`parquet/options_tbbo/root=R/`) includes user-defined spread instruments (UD spreads) which have **negative prices** — the smile fit should use `parquet/options_tbbo_by_expiry/root=R/expiry=D/` (outright-only, joined to definitions) instead.
- `symbology.resolve(stype_in='parent')` places children in the `partial` list, not `result` — counting `result` reads 0 mappings; this is a known SDK behavior, not a data error.
- Timestamp handling: use `ts_event` (matching-engine time), not `ts_recv`; bar alignment should use `db_common.kalshi_bar_end()` which implements the ceil-minute logic against Kalshi's `end_period_ts`.
