## 1. FINDINGS_SYNTHETIC.md — The Original 24-Knot Study

### Purpose
The foundational synthetic validation of the density extractor (Act III Bayesian pipeline). Plants known true densities into real CME option chains with simulated noise, runs the full pipeline, and checks whether the posterior credible intervals contain the truth at the claimed frequency. **Superseded** in numbers (but not verdict) by `FINDINGS_SYNTHETIC_V2.md`.

### Setup Summary
- **Basis:** 24-coefficient cubic B-spline over ±7 vol-scales
- **Planted densities:** crude-like skew (3-lognormal mixture), Black-76 lognormal, bimodal (2.6 vol-scales apart), close bimodal (1.6), Student-t ν=3, Laplace (kinked peak), lognormal + narrow spike outside prior
- **Strike grids:** real CME OTM chains, 8–31 strikes
- **Noise model:** Gaussian with sd = half-spread, floored at half-tick; bid/ask rounded to $0.01
- **Sampler:** 4 chains × 800 NUTS draws; τ integrated out analytically; Gauss-Newton whitening
- **Brackets:** 26 Kalshi $1-wide brackets ±12 around F0 + 2 open tails

### Caveats and Methodological Concerns
- **Sampler quality below stated bar:** Bracket R̂ max median 1.014 (target < 1.01), ESS median 303 (target ≥ 400); divergences in 98% of runs
- **Stage 6 se is 25% too small** due to tick rounding and 1¢ bid floor violating the Gaussian assumption
- **Hyperprior form is not the culprit** — changing τ hyperprior (half-Cauchy, lognormal sd 1, sd 2.5) doesn't move the bimodal trough z (−9.3 / −9.5 / −9.6)
- **Halving noise makes bimodal worse** (C05: trough z +10.9, coverage 0%) — tighter quotes shrink bands faster than they move estimates
- Sub-cent brackets under thin-tailed truth are under-covered (~70%), but bracket errors ≤0.1¢ are irrelevant to 5–20¢ trades

### Potential Sources of False Negatives
- Any real chain with a bimodal true density would produce systematically overstated trough-bracket probabilities with no goodness-of-fit alarm — reads like a persistent, sign-alternating edge across mid-ladder brackets
- Act II disagreement (>8¢) is a latent first-line detector that was not yet operationalised
- χ²/strike passes on mis-specified chains (1.20 vs 1.10 baseline) because many smooth densities price the quotes within spreads

## 4. FINDINGS_TICKFLOOR.md — Tick-Floor Tolerance Sweep

### Purpose
Tests whether flooring the likelihood's tolerance at 1¢ (or variants) improves fit on real chains — and whether it should be applied in the synthetic harness. Real options trade in 1¢ ticks; a 1¢-wide market (half-spread 0.5¢) doesn't locate the price to 0.5¢, yet the writeup treats it as sd = 0.5¢. Task 1 of `HANDOFF_tickfloor_and_fullsynth.md`.

Candidates tested: 0.5¢ in quadrature, 1¢ (max), 1¢ in quadrature, 2¢ (max). All at 48 knots. Exclusion rule (LOO |z| > 3) also tested.

### Key Numerical Results

**Real chains (30 dates, T-1d/T-4h/T-2d snapshots):**

| Snapshot | Treatment | χ²/strike med | Gate 4 fired | Stage 14 pass | Body width (¢) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-1d / 60 min | base48 (no floor) | 1.8 | 27% | 87% | 3.8 |
| T-1d / 60 min | **floor48 1¢** | **1.4** | **3%** | **93%** | **3.2** |
| T-1d / 60 min | excl48 (LOO only) | 1.3 | 0% | 100% | 3.3 |
| T-1d / 60 min | both (1¢ + excl) | 1.1 | 0% | 100% | 2.9 |
| T-4h / 60 min | base48 | 1.5 | 13% | 90% | 7.2 |
| T-4h / 60 min | floor48 1¢ | **1.1** | **7%** | **97%** | **7.1** |
| T-2d / 60 min | base48 | 1.5 | 23% | 96% | 2.7 |
| T-2d / 60 min | floor48 1¢ | **1.2** | **12%** | **96%** | **2.6** |

**Synthetic harness (decision rule applied before runs):**
All floor candidates were **not admissible** because crude-skew coverage drifted above the [88%, 95%] allowed range:

| Floor candidate | Crude-skew 90% cov | Admissible |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5¢ quadrature | 96% | ✗ (too high) |
| 1¢ max | 97% | ✗ |
| 1¢ quadrature | 98% | ✗ |
| 2¢ max | 98% | ✗ |

Stale/crossed quote detection: unaffected by floor. Bimodal coverage: within 5 points of base48. R̂: ≤ 1.03.

**LOO exclusion rule vs floor (§3):**
- Floor brings χ² from ~1.8 to ~1.4 on T-1d chains; LOO brings it to ~1.3 but by deleting 8–10% of quotes
- LOO still fires on 87% of T-1d floored chains — a residual of genuinely inconsistent quotes the floor cannot explain
- Combined (floor + LOO) reaches χ² ~1.1 and Gate 4 0%, removes ~6.7% of quotes

### Verdict
**Adopt 1¢ floor for real chains; do not adopt it as a synthetic harness default.** Decision: `hs_floor = 0.01` (max); LOO exclusion rule kept as a *diagnostic* (flag, don't delete) by default.

### Caveats
- The harness cannot arbitrate the floor's value because its noise model has no tick-level inconsistency; this is a named limitation
- 26–32% of real quotes are 1¢-wide (half-spread 0.5¢); 20–23% are 2¢-wide — the floor addresses a real structural feature
- Some T-1d chains (e.g., 2026-02-27, 2026-04-10, 2026-05-01, 2026-07-10) retain 4–8 LOO flags even under the floor: the floor and exclusion rule are partially redundant, partially complementary
- 2026-04-10 T-1d: floor yields 3 modes (multimodal) vs 1 mode with exclusion — the floor and the rule can give qualitatively different shapes on ambiguous chains

### Potential False Negatives
- Chains where real quotes are inconsistent by more than 1¢ (e.g., a stale quote at 2¢ below neighbours) will not be caught by the floor alone; the LOO residual detector remains necessary
- Without the exclusion rule as default, 10% of T-1d chains may have a systematically high χ² that goes unreported unless the flag count is checked

## 5. FINDINGS_PRIOR.md — Prior Form and Basis-Resolution Study

### Purpose
Systematic test of whether the bimodal blind spot is a **prior-form problem** (Gaussian tails on second differences) or a **basis-resolution problem** (too few knots to represent the trough). Tests Student-t ν=3 and ν=1, horseshoe, and Gaussian at 24 / 36 / 48 / 64 knots, plus the LOO exclusion rule and tick-floor variants. All against the same seeds/noise as the primary study. Task Part A of `HANDOFF_prior_fix.md`.

### Key Numerical Results

**The resolution sweep (bimodal trough):**

| Candidate | Trough z med | Trough width (¢) | Trough cov | Bimodal body cov | Kinked-peak body cov |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gaussian, 24 knots (base) | +6.8 | 0.69 | 0% | 41% | 53% |
| Student-t ν=3, 24 knots | +6.4 | 0.68 | 5% | 40% | 52% |
| Student-t ν=1, 24 knots | +6.7 | 0.70 | 10% | 49% | — |
| Horseshoe, 24 knots | +7.6 | 0.61 | 10% | 34% | — |
| **Gaussian, 36 knots** | **+2.3** | **1.22** | **27%** | **70%** | 82% |
| **Gaussian, 48 knots** | **+1.3** | **1.55** | **57%** | **74%** | 89% |
| Student-t ν=3, 48 knots | +1.5 | 1.75 | 55% | 80% | 90% |
| **Gaussian, 64 knots** | **+1.1** | **1.93** | **75%** | **81%** | 90% |

**Why prior form fails at 24 knots:**
With 24 knots over ±7 vol-scales, knots are 0.7 vol-scales apart. The planted bimodal humps are 2.6 vol-scales apart; the trough spans ~2 knot intervals. A cubic B-spline with 0.7 vol-scale spacing **cannot represent the trough at all** regardless of penalty form. Student-t ν=3 on 24 knots just produces a smaller τ (3.43 vs 4.95) — same trough z, same wrong depth.

**The resolution ladder (smooth truth — crude-skew — coverage held, RMSE rises):**

| Knots | Body cov (A_crude) | Body RMSE (¢) | Body width (¢) | Bracket R̂ | Bracket ESS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 24 | 95% | 0.57 | 1.90 | 1.016 | 290 |
| 36 | 95% | 0.64 | 2.41 | 1.021 | 231 |
| 48 | 95% | 0.66 | 2.70 | 1.020 | 260 |
| 64 | 97% | **0.77** | 3.19 | 1.021 | 217 |

64 knots is **rejected** because A_rmse_body exceeds tolerance (0.77 > 0.57 + 0.10 = 0.67).

**Alternative prior candidates — verdicts:**

| Candidate | Verdict | Reason for rejection |
|---|---|---|
| Student-t ν=3, 24 knots | No improvement | Basis too coarse; trough z unchanged |
| Student-t ν=1, 24 knots | **Rejected** | Regresses A_rmse_body, S08 coverage, R̂, ESS |
| Horseshoe, 24 knots | **Rejected** | Regresses all coverage items; R̂ 1.62, ESS 27 |

| Gaussian, 48 knots | **Partial (2/5 improve)** | Recommended default |

| Gaussian, 64 knots | **Rejected** | Regresses A_rmse_body (0.77 vs 0.57 + 0.10 tolerance) |
| 48 knots + LOO exclusion | Partial | Same bimodal coverage as 48 knots alone; no improvement |

**Tick-floor variants on harness (all partial, same 2 of 5 targets met):**
The floor helps the bimodal coverage (85–92% all-region at 1¢ and 2¢) but drifts crude-skew above the [88%, 95%] admissibility range — same issue as `FINDINGS_TICKFLOOR.md`.

**Sampler health summary:**
- Horseshoe and ν=1: unusable (R̂ 4.16/1.14, ESS 13/37 on crude chains)
- ν=3 at 24 knots: R̂ 1.053, ESS 198 — marginal
- ν=3 at 48 knots: R̂ 1.030, ESS 169 — rejected on R̂ threshold
- Gaussian 48 knots: **R̂ 1.020, ESS 260** — accepted

### Verdict
**48 uniform knots with Gaussian prior is the new default.** Blind spot narrowed: bimodal trough z +6.8 → +1.3, trough coverage 0% → 57%, kinked-peak body coverage 53% → 89%. **Not closed:** trough coverage 57% still short of 80% bar.

The `FINDINGS_SYNTHETIC.md` FAIL verdict is superseded in its diagnosis (prior form was wrong) but not in its status (still FAIL on the same three core criteria).

### Caveats and Open Items
1. **Why the trough is still short:** The band at the trough is ~2× too narrow; the posterior still overweights smooth shapes. Would require knots dense where strikes are dense (divided-difference penalty for non-uniform knot spacing) or an explicit bimodal mixture alternative
2. **Spike truth (G_spike_outside_prior)** is at 59% across all knot counts — essentially flat. Won't improve without non-uniform knot spacing. Deliberately parked
3. **64 knots** gives trough coverage 75% but is rejected for RMSE regression. There may be a coverage-RMSE tradeoff curve worth exploring with a different penalty
4. **The horseshoe's failure** confirmed the docstring warning: a global τ on top of local λ_j creates a flat ridge to τ→0, λ→∞ that NUTS cannot traverse; removed before fitting
5. **Part B (LOO exclusion rule):** On the synthetic harness, the 48 + LOO variant removes 0.7% of clean quotes and 100% of injected stale/crossed quotes — the rule removes noise, not signal, but adds nothing to the bimodal blind spot

### Potential Sources of False Negatives
- Any shape requiring local concentration (spike, bimodal trough, sharp kink) remains underrepresented; the posterior reports uncertainty only among smooth shapes it can represent
- The Act II vs Act III disagreement (8.6¢ vs 0.6¢ on smooth) is the most sensitive real-time alarm for mis-specification, but it is not yet in the production gating logic

## Cross-Cutting Observations

### The Bimodal Blind Spot — Summary Trajectory

| Study | Knots | Trough z | Trough cov | Bimodal body cov | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FINDINGS_SYNTHETIC.md | 24 | +7.8 | 3% | 42% | FAIL |

| FINDINGS_PRIOR.md (64 kts) | 64 | +1.1 | 75% | 81% | REJECTED (RMSE) |

The bimodal blind spot is a structural feature of the smoothness prior + B-spline basis. No sampler tuning, prior form change, or tick-floor variant closes it without a different basis structure.

### What Consistently Works
- **Smooth, single-humped densities:** calibrated at all knot counts across all noise configurations
- **Fault detection:** 100% on forward mis-specification (≥5¢), truncated grid, stale/crossed quotes, unconverged chains — unchanged across all variants
- **Stage 14:** consistently passes — the posterior mean lands on the planted forward with no constraint applied
- **Act II as cross-check:** median max |Δ| 0.56–0.64¢ on smooth truth, 8–12¢ on injected fault chains — a reliable alarm signal

### Recommended Next Steps (from documents)
1. **Non-uniform P-spline** with divided-difference penalty, knots dense where strikes are dense — would address both bimodal trough and spike
2. **Explicit bimodal mixture alternative** (two-lognormal alongside the spline, with stacking/Bayes factor) — would widen bands when bimodal shape is better supported
3. **Operationalise Act II vs Act III disagreement** as a first-line shape-alarm gate in production
4. **Fix Stage 6 se** (inflate by residual MAD ratio, or fix D from the rate curve) before Stage 14 constraint is trusted at 1¢ resolution
5. **Tick-inconsistency noise model** in the synthetic harness — would allow the harness to arbitrate the tick floor's value rather than relying solely on real-chain evidence
